# Key Findings

## 1. Departmental Workforce Concentration

The analysis shows that the workforce is concentrated in a small number of departments.

Shipping has 45 employees, representing 42.06% of the workforce.

Sales has 34 employees, representing 31.78%.

Together, these two departments represent 73.84% of the workforce.

This represents a substantial concentration of employees in two departments, although additional operational data would be necessary to determine whether this concentration represents a business risk.

---

## 2. Geographic Workforce Distribution

The geographic analysis evaluates workforce distribution at two levels:

* Country within region
* Region within the company

The resulting structure allows the relative importance of each country within its region to be distinguished from the overall contribution of the region to the company's workforce.

Geographic concentration should therefore be interpreted at both levels rather than relying on country-level employee counts alone.

---

## 3. Departmental Job Composition

The analysis identified different levels of job concentration across departments.

### Sales

Sales Representatives account for 85.29% of the Sales department, with 29 employees out of 34.

### Purchasing

Purchasing Clerks account for 83.33% of the Purchasing department, with 5 employees out of 6.

### Finance

Accountants account for 83.33% of the Finance department, with 5 employees out of 6.

### IT

Programmers account for 100% of the IT department.

However, IT contains only five employees, demonstrating why percentages should be interpreted alongside absolute counts.

### Shipping

Shipping has a different structure. Stock Clerks and Shipping Clerks each represent 44.44% of the department, while Stock Managers represent 11.11%.

The department is therefore concentrated across two major operational job roles rather than being dominated by a single role.

### Marketing

Marketing is evenly distributed between Marketing Representatives and Marketing Managers, with each representing 50% of the department.

---

## Overall Finding

The workforce structure shows meaningful concentration at both departmental and job-role levels.

The analysis provides evidence of organizational concentration, but concentration itself should not be interpreted as a problem without additional information about workload, operational performance, employee turnover, absenteeism and role criticality.
