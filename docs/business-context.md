# Business Context

## Background

Management requires a clearer understanding of the organization's current workforce structure.

The available HR data contains information about employees, departments, job roles and geographic locations, allowing the workforce to be examined from multiple organizational dimensions.

## Business Need

The objective is to understand whether the workforce is broadly distributed or concentrated across specific departments, geographic areas and job roles.

This information can help management identify organizational patterns that may warrant additional investigation.

## Analytical Objective

The analysis aims to:

1. Measure workforce concentration across departments.
2. Examine geographic workforce distribution across countries and regions.
3. Analyze job composition within departments.
4. Identify structural patterns that may require further business investigation.

## Scope

The analysis focuses exclusively on the current workforce represented in the `EMPLOYEES` table and its relationships with departments, jobs and geographic information.

Historical workforce movements are outside the scope of this analysis.

## Expected Outcome

The final analysis should provide a descriptive view of workforce structure and identify areas where concentration or dependency may deserve further investigation.
